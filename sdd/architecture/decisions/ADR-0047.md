# ADR-0047 — v3 shell polish: icon rail, compact `지금 할 일`, movable open-work button, Office window coordinates and mini chat

Status: Accepted (2026-10-14, CEO). Applies to v3 screens; the open-work button change is shared by v1/v2/v3. Owners: [GC-03 공유 대화 작업공간](../../goalConversation/GC-03-workspace.md), [진행 표시 정책](../../progress-visibility.md), [Security](../../security.md).

## Context

After ADR-0046 the CEO used v3 on Windows and asked for five changes: the left menu takes width the conversation needs; the `지금 할 일` recovery card is too large inside the conversation; the `열린 작업` button covers content and cannot be moved; characters in the Office window sit slightly off from the same characters on the Office page; and the CEO wants to talk to a single teammate from the Office window.

## Decision

1. **Icon rail.** The v3 left menu is a 64 px rail of icons (홈: chat bubble, 업무: board columns, Office: people, 토큰: gauge, 설정: gear, project: folder). Hovering or focusing an icon shows a card with that menu's name to the right of that icon only; the rail width never changes. Each icon keeps its name as the accessible label. v1/v2 keep the full sidebar.
2. **Compact `지금 할 일`.** In the goal conversation the recovery and not-started cards become one bar above the progress line: `지금 할 일 · <title> · 자세히 ›`. The bar opens a dialog with the full reason, facts and the same actions. The conversation and composer stay usable; the dialog closes with Escape, its close button or a click outside. The plan card is unchanged: approval keeps its full scope inside the stream ([ADR-0046](ADR-0046.md) decision 4).
3. **Movable open-work button.** The button can be dragged (left button, 5 px threshold so a click still opens it) or moved with Alt+arrow keys. Its place is kept on this computer (`localStorage`, display preference only) and clamped into the window; the panel opens toward the free side. The default position sits above the composer's send button.
4. **Office coordinates are map coordinates.** Saved character coordinates (0–100) are mapped onto the drawn floor map, not onto the stage box. The map is drawn at a whole-number zoom when it fits and scaled down when the stage is smaller, and the character marker keeps its centering transform in the window. Dragging converts back through the same map rectangle. The Office page and the Office window therefore put a character on the same floor spot.
5. **Mini chat in the Office window.** Clicking a character or a member row opens a small chat card inside the window for that teammate. It sends a read-only question with the existing `ask_goal` command on the selected goal and shows that teammate's latest turns of this goal. It never approves, redirects, revises or starts work, and its turns are the same records the goal conversation shows. Without a selected execution goal with a project, or without an AI connection on the teammate, the card says why instead of offering input.

## Alternatives

- **Rail that expands as a whole on hover:** rejected. It moves the content edge and covers the goal list; a per-item card names one item without layout change.
- **Keep the recovery card inline but collapsed:** rejected. A collapsed card still takes a stream slot and scrolls away; a fixed bar above the composer is always found.
- **Route the mini chat through `send_goal_message`:** rejected. That path can turn a message into a direction change; a side window should not redirect work without the conversation's checks. Direction changes stay in the goal conversation.
- **Separate per-character chat sessions:** rejected. A second record would split the goal's history; using goal turns keeps one record.

## Consequences

- Screen only; no backend protocol change (`ask_goal` exists).
- Code: `v2/src/V2Navigation.tsx` (`compact` rail), `src/GoalChat.tsx` (todo bar and dialog), `src/OpenWorkLauncher.tsx` (`clampLauncher`, drag, stored place), `src/office/OfficeCanvas.tsx` (map rectangle), `src/OfficeMiniChat.tsx`, `src/officeWindowState.ts` (`miniChatBlock`, `miniChatTurns`), `v3/src/App.tsx`, `v3/src/v3.css`, `src/ux.css`.
- QA-45 gains checks for the rail, the todo dialog, dragging the open-work button, character positions in the window, and the mini chat.
