# ADR-0050 — The PM maintains the project's U-SDD

Status: Accepted (2026-10-15, CEO). Owners: [SI-03 SDD 출처](../../specImport/SI-03-origin.md), [Security](../../security.md).

## Context

ADR-0044/0045 create `.ucompany/sdd/` and an AGENTS.md block telling agents to update the task checkboxes, `00_index.md`, `graph.json` and `handoff.md` after work. In the app nobody did: CTO and PM prompts never mentioned the SDD, plans never included SDD files, and a teammate who followed AGENTS.md changed undeclared files, which integration refuses; shared files (`00_index.md`, `graph.json`) cannot belong to two parallel tasks either. On 2026-10-15 the CEO found a sample project's SDD still a skeleton after a goal and decided the PM should manage the SDD by default.

## Decision

1. **Reading.** When `.ucompany/sdd/` exists, CTO and PM get its index, constitution, handoff and the focus feature's next task, and are told to follow it. Plan tasks may name SDD documents as evidence but never list `.ucompany/sdd/` files in `files`.
2. **Writing, by the PM, twice per goal.** After the plan is approved and before implementation (`plan`): `features/<slug>/` with feature.md, requirements.md (`## REQ-###`), plan.md and tasks.md (`- [ ] T-### … (implements: REQ-###)`), graph.json nodes/edges and focus, the index table, handoff. After QA passes (`close`): task checkboxes, a decision record when warranted, handoff (done, open risks, next step), index and graph. A placeholder constitution gets a draft marked `(초안, CEO 확인 필요)`.
3. **Scope and safety.** Each step runs once per goal and phase in an isolated workspace like any task. Only changes under `.ucompany/sdd/` are applied; any other change refuses the whole step. The approval scope says the PM will maintain the SDD. The app refreshes only its own AGENTS.md block to the new wording (PM manages; other teammates read only).
4. **Never blocks the goal.** An SDD step failure is recorded (`sdd_update_failed`) and shown as one conversation line; the work goes on.

## Alternatives

- **App writes the SDD from records:** rejected by the CEO in favour of the PM; content such as requirements and decisions reads better when the planning lead writes it.
- **Each teammate updates its own checkbox:** rejected; shared files across parallel isolated workspaces conflict at integration.
- **SDD as an ordinary plan task:** rejected; the PM would have to plan it every time and a writer other than the planning lead would own it.

## Consequences

- Engine: `run_sdd_step`, `sdd_step`, `integrate_sdd`, `workspace_changes` in `engine.rs`; SDD context in CTO/PM prompts; `validate_plan` refuses SDD files; approval note. `sdd_origin.rs`: `exists`, `planning_context`, `refresh_managed_block`, new managed block wording. `platform.rs`: `folder_changes`. Two PM calls per goal in SDD projects.
- Screen: conversation lines for `sdd_updated` / `sdd_update_failed`.
- QA-48.
