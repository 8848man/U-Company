# ADR-0002 — Separate roles, runtimes, and models

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-10-01

Affects: [Integrations](../../aiIntegration/00_index.md), [Domain](../../domain/00_index.md)

Context: A company role must survive changing providers, and a bare model does not necessarily run tools.

Decision: Persist role, runtime, and model as separate concepts joined by a versioned adapter contract.

Alternatives: Binding a role to a vendor makes switching costly; treating every model as an agent misstates its capabilities.

Consequences: Discovery must test capabilities and assignments may be unavailable until a suitable harness is configured.
